Level 1Fruit Flood

Fresh fruit is bobbing in the glass and the water keeps rising. Burst it all before it floats over the rim!

  • Drag across 3 or more touching fruits of the same kind, then let go to burst them.
  • Longer chains make juicier blasts. Chain 6 or more to get a bubble bomb, then tap it.
  • Every burst pumps water out, and less fruit means lower water.
  • Tap the water to nudge fruit. Tap a fruit to make it hop. Hit DRAIN in an emergency.
